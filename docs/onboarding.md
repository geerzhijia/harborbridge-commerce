# Owner facts needed before research
- Residence/business jurisdiction, selling marketplace and currency; US Amazon is only a provisional assumption.
- Initial inventory and total launch budget, acceptable loss, timeline and desired margin.
- Product interests/exclusions, dimensions/weight targets and categories requiring specialist review.
- Seller Central account and entity status; importer of record plan.
- Available access to research platforms, sourcing accounts, carrier data and authorized integrations. Enter credentials only into secret managers, never chat or this repo.
- Owner approval channel and desired reporting cadence.

An LLC is a later owner decision based on jurisdiction, liability, tax and operating facts. A working company name does not establish an entity, DBA, trademark rights or tax registration. Until reviewed, do not represent the company as an LLC.
